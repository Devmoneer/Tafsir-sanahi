A little more about this project

This community project makes the prayer schedules in its city data files easy to browse. It is free to use, and everyone is welcome to help improve it.

01

Choose your city

Pick a city from the menu to load its schedule directly from the project data.

02

Select a date

Use the date picker to find the available daily times for the date you need.

03

Help it grow

Contribute corrections, data sources, and new cities through the open-source repository.

i

A note on accuracy — The schedules are displayed as stored in this repository. Their calculation method, source, and time zone are not specified here, so please verify important times locally.
